    Northridge College Stadium
    Home of the Wolves · Closed to the public

    NorthridgeAfter Dark

    The Wolves moved out years ago, but the stadium is still locked up and watched every night. Get inside, work your way through all three sections, get down to the field, and kick a field goal under the lights.

    1. Find a way in. Every night a different gate or loading dock is left open. Walk up to a door and try it.
    2. Search the rooms for the field keycard and a bag of footballs. They're somewhere new each run.
    3. Get down to the field through a field gate or the players' tunnel. Both need the keycard.
    4. Kick the field goal from the marked spot. The kick is loud, so make it count.
